How importing with us works

  1. Choose. Pick a vehicle and budget from the price list, or tell us what you need.
  2. Deposit. Pay the agreed deposit so we can bid on your behalf at Japanese auctions.
  3. Win and inspect. We send you the auction sheet, photos and the final purchase price.
  4. Ship. The vehicle is exported and shipped RoRo to your chosen port.
  5. Transit and clear. Road transit to the border, then customs clearance and duty payment.
  6. Register and collect. We help with registration and number plates, then hand over your keys.
